Our approach

From evidence to action, in four steps.

We start from the outcome you need and work backward, with a decision at the end of every step.

1

Diagnose

Agree the outcome, baseline and decision rights. Find the real constraint in demand, offer, pricing, distribution or execution.

2

Test

Probe one important uncertainty cheaply, such as customer interest, price response or partner appetite.

3

Pilot

Run the promising approach in the market with real customers, partners and teams, and track adoption and economics.

4

Scale or stop

Decide with evidence, hand off to a permanent owner, and document what was learned.

A test shows whether an idea earns the next step. A pilot shows how a repeatable model performs in real conditions. We label the evidence honestly, including what has not yet been proven.
