Purpose & Scope

This is a relationship brief on Kevin Hourigan, prepared so Devin can get to know him and his company after a YPO-related meeting of ten attendees. Primary Finding

Kevin Hourigan is a 30-year digital agency founder who merged his company into a larger one and stayed on as President. He began in digital advertising in 1996, grew his company from 3 to 225 people, saw it shrink to about 20 after the dot-com bust (per an Inspired Insider summary) and rebuilt it as Bayshore Solutions in Tampa. On January 1, 2021 it merged with Iowa-based Spinutech to form Spinutech, LLC, with Marc Reifenrath as CEO and Kevin as President and about 160 experts. His public voice is practical and agency-focused: weekly LinkedIn posts on AI search, tech-stack lock-in and web design; two podcasts; and conference appearances alongside Progress Sitefinity. He has also served YPO since about 2012, and community boards in Tampa. The open question is what he wants next: AI-search growth for Spinutech, a bigger role in YPO, M&A, or his own succession.

Scope & Identity Check

This audit covers Spinutech (spinutech.com), a full-service digital agency formed on January 1, 2021 when Cedar Falls, Iowa-based Spinutech, Inc. merged with Tampa-based Bayshore Solutions. The email kevin@spinutech.com and Kevin Hourigan’s LinkedIn profile (President) tie the person to the company. The profile and the company sources differ on a few points: LinkedIn calls him “president and co-founder” and its Experience entry still carries the Bayshore Solutions description (founded 1996, 2,600 clients in 54 countries), while the company History page says Spinutech was founded in 2000 by three University of Northern Iowa students and the merger announcement names Marc Reifenrath as CEO and Kevin as President. Headcount also varies by source (100+ on LinkedIn, 110 on Built In, 170+ on the History page). A companion person audit on Kevin Hourigan sits in the same folder.

Primary Finding

Spinutech is a 25-year-old-plus digital agency built by merger, now repositioning around AI-era search and web performance. The January 2021 combination of Spinutech (Iowa) and Bayshore Solutions (Tampa and Denver) created what Hourigan called “one of the largest, privately held, pure digital agencies in North America,” with about 160 experts and a projected $33M in 2021 revenue. The firm lists Google Premier Partner, Meta Business Partner and Google Marketing Platform status, Inc. 5000 appearances from 2018 to 2022, a partnership with Profound for AI search visibility and participation in Progress Sitefinity’s MartechNEXT events. Clients on the site include Whole Foods Market, Welltower, Hy-Vee, Yanmar, Panavision, Delta Dental and Kalsec; Everglades Boats is a recent award-winning redesign. The weak points are on the public face: the site shows stale content (a 2023 webinar, a 2023 copyright), no named leaders on the pages fetched, conflicting headcount and office information, and results that are mostly framed as design or process rather than numbers. The constraint is converting a broad web, SEO and development bench into a sharper AI-search and revenue-growth offer with proof.
