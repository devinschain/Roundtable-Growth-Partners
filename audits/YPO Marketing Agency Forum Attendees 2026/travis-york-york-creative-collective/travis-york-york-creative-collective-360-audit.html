Primary Finding

Travis York is a New Hampshire-born agency leader who built a group of companies around his agency. He started in early internet marketing for Campbell’s, Celebrity Cruises, Royal Caribbean, Guinness, IBM, Intel and Virgin, holds a U.S. patent for one of the first online lottery platforms (which powered the New Hampshire Lottery for over a decade), rose to president of the Manchester agency then called GY&K in 2010 and has been CEO of GYK since January 2011. He launched York Creative Collective in October 2019 with ventures across marketing, production, consumer brands and, more recently, real estate and hospitality through Queen City Center in Manchester. Three ventures have been sold in 2023–2024. He also describes himself as an active investor and advisor and advises Amber Health, a mental-health support company for the music industry. The open question is what he wants next: more exits, more real estate, growth capital for ventures or a larger YPO role.

Scope & Identity Check

This audit covers York Creative Collective (YCC) (yorkcreativecollective.com), a privately owned group of creative and consumer ventures based in Manchester, NH, and its flagship agency GYK (heygyk.com), formerly GYK Antler and before that GY&K. The email travis@yorkcreativecollective.com and Travis York’s LinkedIn profile (Founder & CEO, Sept 2019 to present) tie the person to the company. Travis York is not Kyle York: Kyle is his brother, founder of York IE and former chief revenue officer at Dyn, and co-invests with him through York Real Estate. The domain gyk.com is for sale and is unrelated. YCC’s homepage names no ventures or clients; ventures come from the Ventures page, and clients come from GYK’s case studies. A companion person audit on Travis York sits in the same folder.

Primary Finding

YCC is a marketing-agency-led holding company. Travis York launched it in October 2019 with Pam Hamlin (former Global CEO of Arnold Worldwide) as President, an agency (GYK Antler, now GYK) as the “growth engine” and eight ventures. It has since sold three ventures in 2023–2024 (4AM Demand to XFactor.IO, Iron & Air to Cole Publishing, TorchCo to Shift Group), added Windy Films (2022), Queen City Studios and Cocktail Academy, and expanded into real estate and hospitality through Queen City Center, a $6.25 million redevelopment at 215 Canal Street in Manchester anchored by Harpoon Brewery. GYK reports clients including the New Hampshire Lottery, Ken’s Foods, Sweet Baby Ray’s, Sallie Mae and the Boston Bruins, and a Boston Business Journal 2026 “Best Place to Work” award. The weak points are public clarity: the YCC homepage names no ventures, clients or leaders, no revenue or headcount is public for the group, and the model has shifted from tech and media ventures to production, consumer brands and real estate. The constraint is explaining what YCC is today and what a venture or client gets from it.
