Primary Finding

Marshall Lockton is a second-generation insurance-brokerage operator turned family-office principal and agency Chair. After roughly thirteen years in Lockton’s operations group (the world’s largest privately owned insurance brokerage, where he is still a shareholder and board observer), he founded Meraki Investments in 2022 and bought Knight Agency in September 2023 as its first acquisition. Meraki wants majority equity in founder- and family-owned B2B and employer-services businesses in the Central US with $5M+ revenue and $1–5M EBITDA. He also hosts The Third Layer, a family-business culture podcast, and serves on the Big Brothers Big Sisters of Kansas City board. For Devin, a 30-year founder, investor and board member who has sold four companies, the relevance is direct: Marshall is an active buyer building a platform, with a stated interest in complementary capabilities to Knight, and he is a peer in purpose-led, long-hold investing.

Scope & Identity Check

This audit covers Knight Agency (knightagency.com), an engagement agency in Orlando and Kansas City owned by Meraki Investments. The email marshall@knightagency.com and Marshall Lockton’s LinkedIn profile (Partner/Chair, Oct 2023 to present) tie the person to the company. A companion person audit on Marshall Lockton sits in the same folder.

Primary Finding

Knight is a 30-year-plus engagement agency repositioned around “people and purpose,” now owned by a family office and building a thought-leadership engine around storytelling. Meraki Investments acquired it on September 29, 2023 (MS Capital advised), and Marshall Lockton became Partner/Chair. Its offer is culture transformation, learning and development, and brand marketing for clients such as Mastercard, Coca-Cola Consolidated, ABM, ASU Prep Digital, Holiday Inn and the US Open. Partner Danielle Krischik wrote The Story Effect, hosts a podcast that reached episode 50 in about two years, and was profiled in Forbes on November 25, 2025. The market tailwind is real: Gallup reports global engagement at 20%, the lowest since 2020, and manager engagement down to 22%. The weak points are proof and visibility: no named team, awards or testimonials on the site, reach numbers instead of business outcomes, a Lockton client relationship that overlaps with ownership, and a small bench (about 30 staff per Winmo). The constraint is turning thought leadership into a repeatable, measurable offer.
