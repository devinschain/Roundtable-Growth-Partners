Primary Finding

Marty Skotnicki is an ad-tech sales executive turned co-founder and President of a company that resells and manages The Trade Desk for mid-market advertisers. Before Mogl he held senior roles at Adconion, Amobee, zeotap and Emodo (SVP Sales at Emodo, 2018 to July 2021), then became President of what was then Ventura Growth in December 2021. His public voice is almost entirely about the company: event invitations with The Trade Desk, hiring calls, awards and team summits. He is a CU Boulder marketing graduate who lived in New York for 16 years, moved to Colorado about six years ago and competes in endurance sports (Leadville MTB 100, the Gran Fondo World Cycling Championship). The Mogl leadership page also names a co-founder and Chairman, Andrew Fischer, so he is one of two founders.

Executive Summary

A strategic assessment of Mogl — the Denver-based Trade Desk Certified Service Partner, formerly Ventura Growth, serving independent agencies and growth brands.

Visit mogl.com ↗
Scope & Identity Check

This audit covers Mogl (mogl.com), formerly Ventura Growth, a Denver-headquartered programmatic and retail-media partner built on The Trade Desk. The email marty@mogl.com and Martin “Marty” Skotnicki’s LinkedIn profile (President & Co-Founder) tie the person to the company. A different company called MOGL (mogl.online, a college-athlete NIL platform led by Ayden Syal) is excluded. Sources disagree on the founding year: Built In says 2021, the Business Wire release and the About page say January 2022, and Martin’s LinkedIn shows him as President from Dec 2021. A job posting that says 2013 carries a note that parts of it are AI-generated and is not used.

Primary Finding

Mogl is a Trade Desk Certified Service Partner that sells mid-market access to the platform, and it is growing fast on a narrow base. The September 2025 release says it supports 500+ advertisers, all exclusively on The Trade Desk, and calls Mogl Growth one of the largest partners in the program. It added retail media (Mogl Retail), data enablement (Mogl Data), a London practice (Mogl UK) and an internal tools group (Mogl Labs), opened a New York office and wins workplace awards (Built In 2026 and Inc Best Workplaces 2026, per company posts). The weak points are on the public face: mogl.com names no clients, shows template placeholder text and still links to Ventura Growth, and the business depends on one platform and one partner program. The constraint is turning platform proximity into a differentiated, defensible brand.
