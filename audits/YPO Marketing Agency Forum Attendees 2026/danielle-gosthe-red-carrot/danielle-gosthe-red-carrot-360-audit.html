Purpose & Scope

This is a relationship brief on Danielle Gosthe, prepared after a YPO-related meeting of ten attendees so Devin can get to know her and her company. Primary Finding

Danielle Gosthe is a fifteen-year founder-operator who built a federal communications agency from an industrial-engineering mindset: University of Miami B.S. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering, a Hispanic woman-owned firm founded in December 2011, and a profile that stresses “outcomes over outputs.” She is at an inflection point. In roughly three months she announced CBP, TSA, Air Force, SBA and CDC awards and a national DHS public-safety campaign, while the firm’s 8(a) status ended in April 2025 and its GSA schedule period ends in April 2027. She is publicly growing and hiring, a YPO member (a shared affiliation with Devin) and visible in the Army, Miami PR and GovCon circles. The best conversation is not about marketing. It is about how to scale delivery, diversify beyond federal, and build a bench without losing the founder-led quality that won the work.
