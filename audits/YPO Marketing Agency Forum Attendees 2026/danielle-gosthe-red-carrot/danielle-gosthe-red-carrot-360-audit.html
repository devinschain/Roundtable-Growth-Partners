Executive Summary

A strategic assessment of Danielle Gosthe and Red Carrot, Inc. — a woman-owned federal strategic communications and marketing agency headquartered in Miami with a Washington, DC presence.

Scope & Identity Check

This audit covers Danielle Gosthe and Red Carrot, Inc. Her LinkedIn profile lists her as Managing Director of Red Carrot (experience entry: Founder/Managing Director, Dec 2011 to present), which matches the company’s About page naming her as founder. The email danielle@theredcarrot.com sits on the same company domain, so the person-to-email link is consistent. Ownership is described as Hispanic woman-owned (Enterprising Women, July 2017; SAM self-certifications), and the WOSB certification implies at least 51% woman ownership, though the exact split is not public. LinkedIn places her in the Washington, DC area while the company lists a Miami, FL headquarters (address records show a move from 1951 NW 7th Ave to 520 Brickell Key Drive). Award headlines quoted in her posts (Orange Slices) were read from the profile and not fetched individually.

Primary Finding

Red Carrot is a founder-led, woman-owned federal communications agency that is in a visible win streak. Within roughly the last three months of Danielle Gosthe’s LinkedIn posts it announced a CBP strategic marketing BPA (headline value $50M, five-year single award, beat three), a TSA talent-acquisition marketing contract ($24M headline, four years, beat 11), a five-year Air Force OC-ALC IDIQ (beat 10), an SBA Office of Disaster Recovery & Resilience communications award (beat six) and a 4.5-year CDC workforce-development contract, on top of the national “If You See Something, Say Something®” campaign. Underneath sit a GSA schedule (GS00F181GA, $54.5M obligated per HigherGov) and three OASIS+ vehicles. The risks are structural: 8(a) graduated April 8, 2025, the GSA schedule period ends April 25, 2027, and the firm now has to staff and deliver several large awards at once as a self-described “small, seasoned team” with no named leaders beyond Gosthe. Hiring (four roles posted on LinkedIn) is the visible answer. The constraint is delivery capacity and showing the bench, not demand.

Strengths
  • GSA MAS plus OASIS+ SB, 8(a) and WOSB
  • Named DHS, HHS, NSF, NOAA and Guard clients
  • Engineer-founder measurement story
  • 14-year history; WOSB certified
  • Seven integrated service lines
Weaknesses
  • No named team beyond the founder
  • Case studies lack results and agencies
  • Award totals conflict across sources
  • Concentration in NAICS 541613
  • Contradictory job posting requirements
Opportunities
  • Capture pipeline for competed work
  • Teaming agreements with primes
  • Leadership and results content
  • Measurement framework as a product
  • OASIS+ WOSB task orders
Threats
  • 8(a) status ended Apr. 8, 2025
  • GSA schedule period ends Apr. 25, 2027
  • Federal budget and contract consolidation
  • Larger agencies with deeper benches
  • Price pressure at recompete
