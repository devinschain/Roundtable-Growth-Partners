Case study 01

ZMC & Associates

Building the Growth Engine from the Ground Up

The situation

ZMC & Associates, an accounting and advisory firm, was ready to scale but lacked the infrastructure to do it. Their brand was dated, their story was diffuse, and there was no clear path from first impression to a qualified conversation. Marketing and sales were not aligned. Every lead follow-up was manual.

What we did

We started with the growth question: where is the next revenue coming from, and what is blocking it? The answer was clarity and structure, not more spend. We sharpened their positioning, rebuilt their visual identity, and stood up a conversion-ready site with offer pages, proof, and clear calls to action. Then we built the funnel: CRM scoring and routing, nurture cadences, SLA alerts, and a weekly operating rhythm tying marketing activity to pipeline.

One story, one funnel, one source of truth.

125%increase in lead generation
5+marketing channels activated in 30 days
42%improvement in return on ad spend

Case study 02

Hampden Watch Co.

From Family Instinct to a Repeatable Affinity Growth System

The situation

Hampden Watch Co. — America's oldest continuously operating watch company, founded in 1922 — had real heritage, a loyal collector base, and early traction in direct and affinity marketing. But the traction was running on instinct. Orders were being closed by hand, more than twenty partner candidates were in consideration with no rubric to rank them, and there was no shared language for what was actually working. The risk was spending energy on the flashy option rather than the sequentially right one.

What we did

We started with structure. In a single working session, we gave the business a shared language it did not have — mapping four affinity models, ranking them by attractiveness, and building the unit economics behind each one. Then we imposed testing discipline: a clear distinction between a test, which isolates one variable at real volume and earns the right to scale, and a pilot, which is directional and tells you where to look next.

We scored over twenty candidate partners against a real rubric and pushed back on the instinct to lead with associations and fraternities. The aviation program was prioritized first because the sequencing made more sense. Alongside the marketing work, we helped Hampden build the external advisory bench a family business often waits too long to seek — vetting partners openly, naming conflicts, and bringing in outside judgment.

Instinct became a system. A lucky quarter became a business line.

20+candidate partners scored and ranked against a structured rubric
4affinity channels mapped with full unit economics
1strepeatable affinity system built from the ground up

Case study 03

Luminate

From Staffing Agency to Scalable Talent Platform

The situation

Luminate was a traditional staffing agency with solid clients and reliable service — but no scale. They had one undifferentiated offering, a brochure website with weak conversion, and no growth funnel. Revenue growth depended entirely on outbound and manual placements. There was a margin ceiling with no clear path through it.

What we did

We repositioned the firm from staffing vendor to tech-enabled talent platform. That meant defining three distinct product offerings with their own ICPs, pricing, and distribution paths. We rebuilt the brand, launched a conversion-first website, and stood up a full funnel — capture, nurture, book — with sales enablement materials and a CRM and dashboard tying marketing to revenue.

Strategic clarity turned into commercial impact.

3new product offerings and revenue sources launched
26%shorter sales cycle through automation
56%increase in average lifetime value per client
