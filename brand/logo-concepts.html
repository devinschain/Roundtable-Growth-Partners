Roundtable Growth Partners: logo concepts

Four directions in Slate & Signal Teal, shown on light, reversed on slate, and small. Final artwork would use a custom serif; Georgia is a stand-in here.

1Seats around the table

A solid table ringed by six equal seats. One seat in teal shows the growth lead without leaving the table. Directly expresses the equal-seat idea.

Reversed, on light, and favicon size

2Open ring

Six equal arcs form a ring around a central table. Calmer and more abstract; reads well at favicon size.

Reversed, on light, and favicon size

3RGP monogram

A serif RGP inside a ring with one teal seat. The most formal option; good for the short name and favicon.

Reversed, on light, and favicon size

4Wordmark

Roundtable in serif, GROWTH PARTNERS spaced out in teal. The quietest, most senior look, with no symbol.
