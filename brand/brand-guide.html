Roundtable Growth Partners: brand guide

Short name: RGP. Palette: Slate & Signal Teal. Idea: everyone has an equal seat at the table.

Logos

Primary: horizontal lockup (rgp-lockup-horizontal.svg)
Reversed, for dark backgrounds
Monogram: avatars, social profiles, favicon
Wordmark: quiet, symbol-free use

Rules: keep clear space equal to the height of the ring's teal dot on all sides; do not recolor, stretch or add effects; use the reversed version on slate or teal. The logo text uses Georgia as a stand-in for Fraunces. Before print or signage, have final artwork converted to outlines with the licensed font.
